Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026 · Applies to StudyHub on Steam and solidillusion.com

Solid Illusion respects your focus and your privacy. This policy explains what limited data StudyHub collects so multiplayer rooms and cloud saves work, how that data is stored, and how you can ask for it to be removed.

1. What StudyHub Collects

When you launch StudyHub through Steam, we store only the account and gameplay records needed to run the app:

  • Steam Account Identifier & Display Name: Your Steam ID and public persona name provided via Steamworks, used to sign you in without requiring a separate password or email.
  • Study Progress & Statistics: Session durations, Pomodoro timers completed, streaks, and HubCoins earned.
  • Customization & Inventory: Your chosen avatar appearance, unlocked wardrobe items, desk items, redeemed gift or creator codes, and Library Card tier.
  • Personal Study Content: Your study journal entries, to-do items, and sticky note settings when synced to your profile.
  • Live Multiplayer State: Your avatar position, seating state, and display name while you are inside a shared library room, plus text chat messages if you choose to send them.

2. No Microphone, No Camera, No Ad Trackers

StudyHub never accesses your microphone or webcam. We do not sell player data, run third-party advertising networks, or embed behavioral ad trackers in the game or on solidillusion.com.

3. Where Data Is Stored

Player profile data, study statistics, customization, and cloud-saved journal records are stored securely on managed PostgreSQL infrastructure hosted by Supabase with row-level access controls. Payment processing for optional Steam in-game purchases is handled exclusively by Valve through Steam; Solid Illusion never sees or stores your credit card or billing details.

4. Website (solidillusion.com)

This website is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no advertising or analytics cookies. If you email us directly or download the Press Kit, standard server transfer logs may be processed by our hosting provider to deliver the requested files securely.

5. Data Retention & Deletion Requests

We keep your StudyHub profile data for as long as your account remains active so your customization, study hours, and journal remain available whenever you return. You can request a copy or permanent deletion of your stored StudyHub data at any time by emailing support@solidillusion.com with your Steam ID. Verified deletion requests are completed within 30 days.

6. Changes to This Policy

If StudyHub adds features that change how data is handled, we will update this page and revise the date at the top.

7. Contact

Questions about privacy or StudyHub can be sent directly to Regina at Solid Illusion via support@solidillusion.com.
