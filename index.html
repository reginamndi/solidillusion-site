What happens inside the library

Simple tools and quiet company for long study nights.

01 / Shared Rooms

Study beside real people, silently

Walk into any floor, pick a seat, and study alongside other students worldwide. There is no voice chat and no webcam, just the calm feeling of a full library.

02 / Desk Tools

Your timer, tasks, and lo-fi in one place

Use the Pomodoro timer, nested to-do list, movable sticky notes, study journal, and built-in lo-fi radio without juggling five extra browser tabs.

03 / Atmosphere

Morning sun, golden hour, or 2 AM rain

Switch the lighting to match your hour across three library floors and the outdoor garden, then customize your avatar's hair, clothes, and colors.

04 / Lightweight

Easy on student laptops

Free on Steam at around ~760 MB with low VRAM usage and 18 languages, so it sits quietly beside your PDFs, code editor, or lectures.
